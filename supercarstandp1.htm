About this stand

You spent hours building the McLaren P1. Laying it flat on a shelf means the engineering LEGO packed into the underside — the V8 engine, 7-speed gearbox, and full suspension geometry — never gets seen.

This stand lifts and tilts the entire car so all of that faces outward. Four cross-braced columns sit on four Technic tires for grip and stability. The cradle is sized for the full 1:8 supercar line, making it a natural fit for the Bugatti Chiron and Porsche GT3 RS with minor adjustments.
