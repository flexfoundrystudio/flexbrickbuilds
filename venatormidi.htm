About This Build

Here is my take on the Venator Midi.

I designed this between the release of the official Acclamator Midi (75404) and the official Venator Midi (75441), so I drew some inspiration from those models — and from several of the many others posted on Rebrickable that I built along the way.

This is scaled perfectly to the 75404, where the official Venator Midi is still a little small. The model is very sturdy, with the exception of the command spires and smaller details. I built this many times over with real parts during prototyping.

The model has a 16L axle with full-length Technic bricks as a backbone. The upper superstructure plates are connected securely with the #7 axle and pin connector (4459), and the lower with hinge bricks. You could replace the trans-light blue tiles with grey to make it more like the official midi sets, or modify the exterior color scheme to your liking. I played around with that a lot and think I found a good balance — really happy with how it turned out. Hope you enjoy!

Instructions are 330 steps in PDF format, made with Studio. If you run into any problems please let me know so I can correct them.
